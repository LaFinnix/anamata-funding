## Eligibility snapshot (Fin's situation)

- ✅ NZ-registered company (Ltd) — meets the basic NZ-entity requirement
  for most NZ On Air / Creative NZ / Te Mātāwai / NZ Music Commission rounds.
- ✅ Music-led output (waiata, Māori-language content) — strong fit for
  Ngā Reo o Te Iwi and Te Mātāwai specifically.
- ⚠️ **Track record** — most rounds want at least one prior released
  project. Anamata has Apple Music singles + a YouTube channel, so
  most first-time rounds will accept this; capability grants don't
  require prior outputs.
- ⚠️ **Māori component** — for Ngā Reo o Te Iwi and Te Mātāwai the
  waiata must be in te reo Māori (or substantially so) and ideally have
  iwi connection. Anamata's catalog already meets the language bar.
- ❓ **Co-funding** — some rounds require matched funding (e.g. you
  put in 30%, they put in 70%). Make sure cashflow is ready.
- ✅ **Cultural review** — Māori language consultant engaged. All
  te reo material has a reviewer before submission. **Update 2026-07-15:**
  consultant confirmed by Fin.

## How the cron uses this file

The funding cron reads this file every Monday and:

1. Checks the open dates of every round listed above.
2. For rounds that are **currently open**, runs an eligibility check
   against the snapshot at the top of this file.
3. Reports back to Telegram: open rounds, eligibility verdict, and
   anything blocking submission.
4. When a round's deadline is **within 14 days**, escalates.
5. When a round **closes without being submitted**, flags the missed
   opportunity and asks if you want to plan for the next round.

## Updating

If Fin's situation changes (new entity type, new releases, co-funding
secured, cultural reviewer confirmed), update the **Eligibility
snapshot** at the top. The cron reads that section first.
