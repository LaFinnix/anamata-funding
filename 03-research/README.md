# Research

The research folder holds background work that informs Anamata's funding strategy.

## What's in this folder

- `OPPORTUNITY-SHORTLIST-2026-07-28.md` — the 7-item funder-driven opportunity shortlist that drove the OPP-04 (impact dashboard), OPP-05 (Kaikōrero collab), OPP-06 (koha reciprocity ledger + 3 methodology papers), and OPP-07 (waiata catalogue) builds on the platform. Source of the rubric-mapped application approach that distinguishes Anamata from generic arts-grant applicants.

## What lives in the platform repo (not here)

The OPP-06 methodology papers — citable as research outputs for Skinner Fund, Ngā Puanga Pūtaiao, and HRC Māori Advancement — live in `anamata-kahui/docs/`:
- `METHODOLOGY-CULTURAL-REVIEW.md`
- `METHODOLOGY-IWI-MANA-RARAUNGA-CARE.md`
- `METHODOLOGY-KOHA-ATTESTATION.md`

They are referenced in funding applications (as "available on request" attachments) but their canonical home is the platform repo.

## When this gets updated

- New funder research sweep → add as `OPPORTUNITY-SHORTLIST-<date>.md`
- New methodology paper → add to platform repo + reference here

The research is the *why* behind the applications. Don't lose track of it.
