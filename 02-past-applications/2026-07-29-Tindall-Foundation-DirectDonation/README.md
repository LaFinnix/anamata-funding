# 2026-07-29 — Tindall Foundation — Direct Donation

## Round

Tindall Foundation Direct Donations pathway (Option #2 on the Tindall
donations-and-eligibility page). Direct Donations support "work that
can be scaled up or has significant regional or national reach."

Tindall accepts applications in English, Te Reo Māori, or NZSL. This
application is submitted in English (the online form's primary
language); a te reo version is available within 24 hours on request.

## Application timeline

- **Application date:** 2026-07-29
- **Submission method:** Online form at https://tindall.org.nz/contact/
- **Expected response time:** Tindall Direct Donation applications are
  processed on a rolling basis; expect 4-8 weeks for an initial
  response. Tindall explicitly cannot support urgent or retrospective
  applications — this is a forward-looking proposal for a 3-month
  workstream.
- **Project period if funded:** August – October 2026 (3 months)

## Funding requested

NZD $9,500 (inclusive of GST). Four costed deliverables:

| Deliverable | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Peer review of the 3 methodology papers (1 Māori data sovereignty academic) | $4,000 |
| CARE conformance benchmark with 3-5 consenting NZ orgs + public report | $3,500 |
| DOI minting for the 3 methodology papers (Zenodo, mostly complete) | $500 |
| Te reo Māori upgrade of CARE Auditor + Provenance Embedder + paper abstracts | $1,500 |
| **Total** | **$9,500** |

These deliverables are also visible at the Tindall landing page
(`/for-funders/tindall`), which serves as the live public-facing
companion to this written application.

## Honest scope

- We do NOT pitch our waiata releases as Tindall-eligible. Tindall's
  exclusions list names "Arts, drama, performing arts, museums, and
  galleries." Our waiata catalog (4 released waiata, 8 published)
  falls under that exclusion and is not the angle of this application.
- This application pitches the *research + cultural-data
  infrastructure* (methodology papers, CARE Auditor, Provenance
  Embedder, kaitiaki rōpū governance, iwi-gate consent log) — which
  IS in scope under Tindall's "Identity and social cohesion" focus
  area + "Mātauranga Māori" giving priority.
- Tindall's 3 priority regions (Te Tai Tokerau, Tairāwhiti, Horowhenua
  & Whanganui) are not directly in our operational footprint. This
  application fits the "Identity and social cohesion" focus area
  rather than the regional priority.
