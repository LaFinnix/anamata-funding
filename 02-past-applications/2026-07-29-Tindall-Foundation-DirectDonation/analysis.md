## Key decisions + trade-offs in this application

### What we INCLUDED

- **3 DOIs in Section 4 (Outcomes)** — These are the strongest possible evidence of citable research artefacts. The DOIs resolve, the papers are at version 1.0 with honest limitations disclosed in each paper's §6.
- **5 live URLs in Section 8 (Attachments)** — Each URL is a real, deployed, public surface. The Tindall assessor can click each one and verify. This is the strongest possible "show, don't tell" move.
- **Honest scope disclosure** — Section 8 explicitly says we don't pitch our waiata releases (Tindall excludes arts). Better to lose a funder over misalignment than waste their reviewer's time on something they'd exclude anyway.
- **Multi-funder strategy disclosure** — Section 9 + the README make clear this is one piece of a larger funding picture. Tindall is the right fit for *this* $9,500 because of the focus area + giving priorities; other funders cover other surfaces.
- **NZ-based hosting + Auckland region** — Tindall prioritises NZ. Our hosting is in Auckland (Supabase region) + Vercel edge. This is a soft positive.

### What we CUT (deliberately)

- **Waiata subject matter** — waiata catalogues, waiata funding history, anything music-domain. Tindall excludes arts.
- **Vague "future plans"** — we have concrete 3-month deliverables, not aspirations.
- **Tindall-specific 3 priority regions** (Te Tai Tokerau, Tairāwhiti, Horowhenua & Whanganui) — we don't have operational presence there. Section 5 explains our national + international reach is the operational evidence surface, not regional service delivery.
- **NZBN + company number** — these were dropped from the boilerplate on 2026-07-29 (privacy posture). Section 6 says "registration details available on request" which is the same disclosure with a softer default.
- **Koha reciprocity ledger details** — would have been off-topic for the Tindall focus area. The ledger is referenced in the platform's `/credits` page but not emphasised here.

### Risks + open questions

- **Risk 1: Tindall assessor may flag the entity-eligibility narrative as insufficient.** Section 6 explains the not-for-profit / for-profit separation but doesn't provide formal accounts. **Mitigation:** the application says "Companies Office registration documents + both sets of accounts are available on request" — flag proactively rather than wait for them to ask.
- **Risk 2: Peer review cost may be underestimated.** $4,000 for 8-10 hours per paper at $150/hr assumes 1 academic at $150/hr. NZ academic rates vary ($100-$300/hr depending on seniority + institution). **Mitigation:** offer a range if Tindall prefers — "$3,000-$5,000 for the peer review workstream."
- **Risk 3: CARE conformance benchmark depends on getting 3-5 consenting orgs.** We don't have any committed yet. **Mitigation:** the application says "3-5 consenting NZ organisations (iwi trusts, GLAM institutions, research bodies)" — if we can't get 3 by project start, we reduce scope. This is honest.
- **Risk 4: Te reo upgrade cost may not cover full native review.** $1,500 for native review of every translation string + re-recording abstracts is tight. **Mitigation:** prioritise the highest-visibility strings (audit form, report headings, methodology paper abstracts) over the long tail.

### What this application is NOT trying to do

- **Not pitching the platform as a finished product.** It's a working platform with documented limitations (the methodology papers' §6 each disclose this honestly).
- **Not asking for ongoing support.** This is a one-off $9,500 Direct Donation for a 3-month workstream, not a multi-year grant.
- **Not competing with Creative NZ or NZ On Air.** The funding landscape is intentionally multi-funder compatible: each funder funds a different surface of the same platform.
