## What this application was for

Two-pillar 12-month capability-building programme:

1. **Cultural Competency** — embedding Te Tiriti o Waitangi and te ao Māori into Anamata's governance, contracts, policies
2. **Accessibility** — Deaf and disabled community inclusion, including NZSL, vibrotactile/haptic production, universal design

The deliverables: gap analysis → staff training (NZQA Level 4 Bicultural Competency + NZSL workshops) → 5-year Accessibility Plan + Accessibility Charter + Māori and Disabled Advisory Board.
