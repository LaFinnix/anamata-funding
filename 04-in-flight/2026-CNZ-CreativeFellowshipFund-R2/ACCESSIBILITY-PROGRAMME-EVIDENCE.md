# Accessibility programme evidence

Compiled 21 September 2026, revised 24 September 2026 against the re-baselined programme files. Every link
in this document was fetched and returned a live page on the compilation date.

## What this document is

The fellowship application says the accessibility infrastructure underneath the music is being built
through a funded programme, and that the fellowship is the artistic research that goes further than that
programme can. This document sets out what that programme is, what can be verified publicly today, what is
ongoing, what independent corroboration exists, and what does not exist yet. The last two sections are the
important ones for an assessor, because one states who can confirm these claims and the other states
plainly what this fellowship would create that nothing else is creating.

## How the two programmes relate

They run in parallel, they interlock on dates, and they do not share a cost.

| | Development Fund programme | This fellowship |
|---|---|---|
| Applicant | Anamata Kāhui Limited | Ngaika Smith as an individual |
| Nature | Organisational capability: audit, training, standards, policy | Artistic research: tactile-first composition, notation, co-composed waiata |
| Period | July 2026 to December 2027 | December 2026 to December 2027 |
| Output | An accessibility standard, a training record, a published plan and charter | A composition method, a notation system, six waiata with tactile scores, an open protocol |

**The two vibrotactile lines, and why they are not this fellowship's work funded twice.** The programme
carries a documented vibrotactile production trial in April 2027 and a mix protocol prototype in June 2027.
Both are organisational deliverables: the trial records what happens when the label applies published
vibrotactile guidance to its own production workflow, and the prototype writes that up as a protocol the
organisation can follow. That programme's own risk treatment requires the work to be framed as protocol
development rather than music production, and requires releases to be funded from a separate source. This
fellowship is that separate source, and it funds different things: the physical rig and its measurement
chain, the empirical test programme, the notation, and the composing. The programme pays for audit,
training, standards and protocol development. No cost line appears in both ledgers, and no hour is claimed
in both programmes.

**Time.** My role in the programme is coordination and reporting rather than research: the audit, the
training and the consulting are delivered by the partners, and the programme's own reporting obligation is
a single report in December 2027. This fellowship is the protected research tenure, and the programme's
administrative load is deliberately structured to sit outside it.

The programme's trial and prototype sit inside the first phase of the fellowship. The fellowship uses them
as a reference point for a shared measurement approach, and then goes where a capability programme is not
designed to go: into how the music itself is written, what a tactile score is, and what happens when a
waiata is composed for the body first.

## What does not exist yet

Stated plainly, because the fellowship is the answer to it.

- No tactile score exists for any waiata, in this catalogue or anywhere else in Aotearoa that I can find.
- No waiata has been composed tactile-first.
- No Deaf artist, NZSL poet or tāngata whaikaha Māori performer has been engaged to co-compose, and none has
  been asked to contribute unpaid time to get this application ready. That is deliberate: engagement begins
  on award, under the collaborator budget in the fellowship ledger, paid at fair pay rates and agreed in
  writing first. Asking people to demonstrate the need before funding exists is how disabled artists end up
  subsidising the applications that are meant to pay them.
- The vibrotactile mix protocol exists as a planned programme deliverable, not as a published standard.
- NZSL and Easy Read production for the platform is committed with target dates, not delivered.
