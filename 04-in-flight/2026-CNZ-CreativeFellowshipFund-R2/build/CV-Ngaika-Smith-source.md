# Ngaika Smith

## Composer and producer · Ngāi Tahu, Ngāti Kahungunu, Ngāti Awa

## Practice

Composer and producer working in te reo Māori, with whakapapa to Ngāi Tahu, Ngāti Kahungunu and Ngāti Awa.
Writes waiata drawn from kōrero tuku iho and leads Te Kāhui Anamata, trading as Anamata Records, a Māori-led
music label and cultural platform. Works across composition, production and the cultural process around
release: cultural review at draft stage, kaitiaki rōpū governance, and trilingual release assets in English,
te reo Māori and New Zealand Sign Language.

Current research interest is the tactile register: composing waiata where the primary version is felt rather
than heard, and documenting the method openly so other composers can use it.

## Cultural practice

Releases carry cultural review at draft stage rather than at the end. The reviewer has been engaged on this
basis, and a kaitiaki rōpū holds governance over programme decisions. Collaborators are contracted and paid
for their time, with rates agreed in writing before work begins. Data and cultural material are held under
the CARE principles: collective benefit, authority to control, responsibility, ethics.

## Accessibility practice

The platform publishes a WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance target, assessed in continuous integration, with its
own known gaps documented publicly: an in-production NZSL video hero, Easy Read variants in development, and
work in progress on forced-colors support. Trilingual production, English, te reo Māori and NZSL, is a
released commitment rather than an aspiration. Vibrotactile mixing, which began as an accessibility
initiative for the label's releases, is the origin of the research in this application.
