# Invitation letter: draft for the partner to put on his own letterhead

This is the strongest single document in the Stage Two application. The partner can sign this in an hour. It is
written so that it stands alone: everything in square brackets is his to fill in, and nothing invented has
been put there. **Do not let a single bracketed placeholder reach the portal.** Check each one is replaced.

The English version is what we file. The Japanese version is what proves the commitment to a Japanese reader.
If the two differ in substance, the English governs and we say so.

---

## Before this goes into the application

1. Every bracketed field is filled in. No exceptions.
2. It is on letterhead, dated, signed, with contact details a funder could use to verify it.
3. It is attached as a PDF, not a screenshot. The portal takes documents, and a photograph of a signed page
   reads as an afterthought.
4. If it is signed by an individual rather than an entity, that is fine, and it is worth adding one line
   confirming he is acting in his own capacity and, if he has a label or publisher, that they are aware.
5. If any specific date or venue is claimed, it is real. An assessor who checks a named festival and finds
   nothing there is the worst outcome this document can produce.
