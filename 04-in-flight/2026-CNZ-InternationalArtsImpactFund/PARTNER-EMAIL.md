# Email to the partner

Send from a working address, keep it short, and put the deadline in his time zone. Everything he needs to
produce is attached, including the invitation letter pre-drafted so he only has to put it on letterhead and
fill the brackets.

---

> ただ声一つ: who owns the master, who owns the composition and lyrics, in what shares, who administers

> putting in, in cash or in kind, with a value. A partner with something in it is a different application
> from a partner who is enthusiastic.

> **5. Anything that shows demand.** Streaming numbers by country, press or radio or playlist placements with
> dates, exploratory emails from programmers or labels, audience data, and the dates and names of the industry
> meetings we have already had. Screenshots are fine.

## What to attach to the email

1. `INVITATION-LETTER-DRAFT.pdf` (English and Japanese in one document)
2. `PARTNER-INFO-SHEET.pdf` (the facts we need, in fill-in form)
3. `PARTNER-REQUEST.pdf` (the full list, if he wants the reasoning behind each item)

Do not attach the internal funding documents, the tracker, or anything containing our own assessment of the
application's weaknesses. Partners get the request, not the working file.
