# IAIF Stage One EOI: submit checklist

**Deadline: 1:00 PM NZ time, Monday 21 September 2026.**
**Where:** the Expression of Interest form linked from
https://www.creativenz.govt.nz/funding-and-support/all-opportunities/international-arts-impact-fund

## The three fields

1. **Applicant details**: copy the table from `STAGE1-EOI.md`, form field 1.
2. **Eligibility check and declaration**: copy form field 2 verbatim. It is written to satisfy the three published tests (international experience, evidence of international demand, current international strategy) without overclaiming on any of them.
3. **Summary of your international experience and goals**: copy form field 3. This is the scored part. It runs to roughly 900 words. If the form caps the field length, the "Who we are" and "International experience" sections are the ones to keep, followed by "Goals and measures"; the three-year programme can be compressed to the year headings plus one line each.

## Verify before you submit

2. "Our third collaboration is in progress" (confirm or delete).
3. Japan as the lead market (change if you want a different first market).

## The eligibility risk you need to close, in writing

Creative New Zealand's published exclusion reads:

> "Artists and practitioners who have applied for funding from the Creative Fellowship Fund for the same period or Creative Impact Fund for the same activity cannot apply to this fund."

You are applying to the Creative Fellowship Fund this week as an individual, and to this fund as Anamata Kāhui Limited, an organisation. The exclusion as published is written against artists and practitioners, and the organisation route is a separate applicant type for a separate purpose (market development and international presentation rather than practice development). That reading is defensible, but it has not been confirmed by Creative New Zealand.

**Do this today or Monday morning.** Send one email, keep the reply:

> Kia ora,
>
> I am submitting an Expression of Interest to the International Arts Impact Fund 2026 on behalf of Anamata Kāhui Limited (NZBN 9429052960734, trading as Anamata Records) as a New Zealand based arts organisation. I am also applying to the Creative Fellowship Fund 2026 Round 2 in my own name as an individual practitioner.
>
> Could you confirm that these are treated as separate applications, given the applicant types differ and the activities are different (practice development in my own name, international market development and presentation through the company)? I want to make sure I am not caught by the exclusion covering artists and practitioners who have applied to the Creative Fellowship Fund for the same period.
>
> Ngā mihi,
> Ngāika Smith
> Founder and Director, Anamata Kāhui Limited
> funding@creativenz.govt.nz

If the answer is that they conflict, the EOI stays filed as the company and the Fellowship is the application that gives way. The Fellowship closes Thursday, so you will have the answer before you have to choose.

## Stage Two, if the EOI passes (closes Thursday 1 October, 1:00 PM)

Three support documents are mandatory and only one of them exists:

| Document | Status |
|---|---|
| Current international strategy | **Does not exist yet. Must be written.** The three-year programme in the EOI is its substance; it needs to become a standalone document with named partners, market analysis and a budget. |
| Evidence of international demand, such as invitations | Partial. The released KUROKORU collaborations are evidence of a working relationship. A written invitation or confirmed presentation is not held. |
| Brief artist or organisation profile showing international experience | Can be assembled from the boilerplate plus the release record. |

Stage Two application questions (four, with published character limits) and the strategy document are the next workstream. Allow two to three days.
