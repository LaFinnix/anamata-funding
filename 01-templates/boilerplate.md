# Anamata Records — Funding Boilerplate

Reusable phrases, lifted or adapted from successful applications.
Use as building blocks when drafting new ones. Always re-read in
context — these are scaffolds, not copy-paste-final.

## Naming origin

> Our name, meaning "the future" or "the time to come," demands
> proactive engagement with the systemic barriers that have
> historically marginalized Māori creatives and Deaf and disabled
> communities.

*(Adapt "Māori creatives and Deaf and disabled communities" if the
round is narrower — e.g. just "Māori creatives" for a music-only
round.)*

> [compliance / quality / accessibility], and hosting focus groups
> with [target community] to identify specific barriers to [their
> participation / engagement].

## Benefits — for the organisation

> For our organisation, this initiative establishes robust internal
> infrastructure and long-term financial resilience. By codifying
> [pillar 1] and [pillar 2] into our operations, we mitigate the risk
> of [cultural / operational / reputational] missteps and ensure
> sustainable governance grounded in te ao Māori principles such as
> rangatiratanga and kaitiakitanga.

## Benefits — for community

> For our communities, particularly [target community], the benefits
> are transformative. We are moving beyond compliance to actively
> champion human rights, ensuring equitable access to [cultural life /
> the arts / music]. By producing trilingual resources in English, Te
> Reo Māori, and NZSL, and pioneering [innovation], we dismantle
> sensory barriers. This allows [community] to fully engage with
> [cultural product].

> The move toward [innovation 1], [innovation 2], and [foundation]

> times but will thrive as a central pillar of community wellbeing.

> create through intentional, culturally grounded, and [universal /
> accessible] actions today.
